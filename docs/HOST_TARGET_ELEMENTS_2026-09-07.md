## Price posture

Do not lead with a number.

If they ask for a proposal before giving a price:
- opening hypothesis: $900/month for Tue-Fri morning access during a 60-day pilot
- comfortable zone: approximately $1,100-$1,300/month if storage and usable hot-line access are included
- initial ceiling: approximately $1,500/month only if the space materially solves wok/hot-line/storage/recurring-access needs

Four 6.5-7 hour mornings/week is roughly 113-121 operating hours/month, so:
- $900 ~= $7.5-$8.0/hour
- $1,200 ~= $10/hour
- $1,500 ~= $12.5-$13.3/hour

That remains materially better than standard commissary economics if access is reliable and equipment is strong.
