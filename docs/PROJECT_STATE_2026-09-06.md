# DAMB GOOD LAH — Project State — 2026-09-06

## Locked decisions

- Canonical name: **DAMB GOOD LAH**.
- Launch architecture: **3 rice + 3 noodles + roti**.
- No soups.
- Launch rice: Padang Chicken Rice, Padang Beef Rice, Nasi Lemak Lah.
- Launch noodles: Dang Good Mee Goreng, Padang Dry Curry Mee, Char Kway Teow.
- Roti is a core product, including a Padang-dip format.
- Sambal Shrimp Rice and Sambal Shrimp Mee move to the expansion/specials bench, not deletion.

## Culinary/operational breakthroughs to preserve

1. **Four flavor lanes remain distinct.**
   - Padang curry is its own lane.
   - House sambal is a mother component with dish-specific finishing.
   - Mee goreng retains a dedicated Mamak-style ketchup/soy/kecap profile.
   - Char kway teow retains dedicated seasoning, chile paste, fresh flat rice noodles and high-heat one-portion testing.

2. **Shared prep without generic food.**
   - One main chicken prep.
   - One shrimp buying spec where practical.
   - One yellow noodle SKU where appropriate; separate fresh flat rice noodle for CKT.
   - Shared potato prep.
   - One firm tofu SKU with tender vs crisp treatments.

3. **One memorable extra per dish.**
   - Chicken-skin crisp.
   - Beef-fat shallot crisp.
   - Ikan bilis-peanut crunch.
   - Crispy potato.
   - Curry tofu crisp.
   - Visible included lap cheong for CKT.

4. **Protect service-time capacity.**
   - Advance-prep dry hitters where safely validated.
   - Limit service-time fryer work.
   - Do not hide station failure by bulk-cooking noodles far ahead of dispatch.
   - Track oldest-food wait, not merely final ticket completion.

5. **Delivery is part of recipe development.**
   - Roti deck vs protected sleeve A/B test.
   - Dry crisp sleeves.
   - 15- and 30-minute delivery endpoints.
   - Two-meal carry test.
   - Noodle clumping and CKT wok-character tests on actual facility equipment.

## Current commercial hypothesis

The biggest economic unknown is not rice or spice cost. It is **sellable main-equivalents per paid kitchen hour**, including prep, service, cleanup and dead time. The current economics model therefore treats throughput as a primary price variable.

## Next evidence gates

1. Measure finished usable yields for all major components.
2. Run low/base/high portion-value tests.
3. Obtain supplier quotes for the highest-spend ingredients.
4. Lock packaging only after delivery testing.
5. Measure all-in paid kitchen productivity and rush throughput separately.
6. Measure roti labor/griddle occupancy.
7. Run channel economics initially with direct ordering and low-commission marketplace options before paying 25–30% solely for reach.
8. Confirm the exact Santa Clara County permitting structure for the chosen commissary/operator arrangement before public sales.
