## HFSAA — what public evidence does and does not establish

HFSAA publicly states that it certifies restaurants, meat markets, dining halls and other food operations. Its published standards require halal-suitable non-meat ingredients in addition to compliant meat.

More importantly, HFSAA's published discussion of partial-halal restaurants identifies cross-contamination risks from non-halal meat, shared cooking oil and shared storage and states that a restaurant must sell only verified halal meat and non-meat products to receive HFSAA certification.

**Interpretation:** DAMB GOOD LAH's own menu architecture is compatible with the stated restaurant standard. The unresolved issue is facility-level shared exposure from other tenants. No public HFSAA source located on 2026-09-07 explicitly says that a separately controlled operator inside a multi-tenant commissary is either always certifiable or always prohibited.

Therefore **written HFSAA preflight remains mandatory before Snell payment.**

## The Commissary — exact questions for the first call/tour

### HALAL / tenant mix

1. Do any tenants currently bring pork into 5475 Snell Ave?
2. Do any tenants handle non-halal chicken, beef or other meat?
3. Which hot equipment is physically shared between tenants?
4. Is fryer oil shared? Can DAMB GOOD LAH maintain a dedicated fryer/oil system or dedicated fryer lane?
5. Can we maintain dedicated wok, pans, utensils, cutting boards, hotel pans and smallwares in separately identified storage?
6. Can we maintain clearly separated/identified refrigerator, freezer and dry-storage areas?
7. What sanitation/reset occurs between tenants using the same station?
8. Will The Commissary permit HFSAA or another halal certifier to inspect/audit the facility and our operating controls?

### Raw-meat / County operation

9. Confirm in writing that renters may receive, store, prep and cook raw chicken, raw beef and raw shrimp.
10. Are renters permitted to batch-cook and rapidly cool curry, braised beef and chicken for later approved reheating/service?
11. Are blast chiller, ice-bath, shallow-pan or other approved cooling workflows available/allowed?
12. Does The Commissary provide the County rental-kitchen agreement / commissary paperwork needed for a renter's permit?
13. Does management know whether current renters operate under `FP18/FP19` caterer/kitchen-rental permits, or another operator structure?

### CKT / equipment

14. What is the make/model and rated BTU/output of the strongest gas burner under the Type I hood?
15. Is there a wok ring or wok range not listed publicly?
16. If not, can DAMB GOOD LAH bring/install a listed commercial wok burner compatible with the hood and fire suppression system?
17. Can we reserve the same wok/range position for recurring lunch production?
18. Will management allow an on-site CKT test before a long commitment?

### Peak access / economics

19. How far in advance are stations reserved on the 20-hour tier?
20. What is actual availability Monday-Friday, roughly 8 AM-1 PM?
21. Can range/wok + fryer + griddle be used simultaneously by one tenant, or are those separate reservable resources?
22. Confirm all mandatory charges beyond $710/mo + $500 onboarding.
23. Confirm insurance limits, cancellation notice, reservation penalties, cleaning charges, oil/grease disposal, utilities and access/key fees.
24. Confirm month-to-month termination notice and whether any minimum initial term exists.
