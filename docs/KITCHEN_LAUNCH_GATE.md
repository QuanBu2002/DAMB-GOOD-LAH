## Hard Gate 1 — HALAL certification feasibility

DAMB GOOD LAH is designed as a fully HALAL operation. No pork enters DAMB GOOD LAH inventory. CKT uses certified halal chicken lap cheong.

Before lease:
1. Ask HFSAA whether a DAMB GOOD LAH food establishment can be certified while operating within a multi-tenant commissary where other tenants may handle pork/non-halal meat.
2. Obtain the exact written controls required for storage, equipment, utensils, fryer oil, receiving, sanitation/reset, labeling, and segregation.
3. Ask whether a dedicated fryer and/or dedicated hot equipment is required.
4. Ask whether shared walk-in refrigeration/freezer shelving is acceptable with approved segregation.
5. Ask whether HFSAA requires any facility-level commitment from The Commissary.
6. Ask whether all processed ingredients must be individually approved before initial certification.

HFSAA contact: info@hfsaa.org
Application: https://www.hfsaa.org/apply

Important: HFSAA publicly emphasizes 100% verified halal restaurant operations and identifies shared storage/oil/equipment as cross-contamination risks. Do not infer that a shared-kitchen arrangement is certifiable until HFSAA confirms our exact setup.

## Hard Gate 2 — CKT heat

CKT remains a protected benchmark. Snell publicly lists a fryer, griddle, ovens and other equipment but does not publicly list a dedicated wok burner.

Before lease:
1. Determine the strongest gas burner available under a Type I hood.
2. Ask whether a commercial high-output wok burner may be installed or brought in.
3. Confirm fire-suppression/hood coverage and facility approval for that equipment.
4. Conduct an on-site one-portion CKT test using the intended wok, fresh flat noodles, shrimp, egg, sprouts/chives and halal chicken lap cheong.
5. Test burner recovery over consecutive portions, not only one isolated wok.

**Failure rule:** if authentic CKT cannot be executed repeatedly on the available/approved equipment, do not simplify CKT to fit the kitchen. Either solve the equipment issue or move to a backup kitchen.

## Hard Gate 3 — Peak equipment access

## Decision rule

Choose the **lowest-fixed-cost kitchen that passes ALL of these gates**:

1. HALAL certifier accepts the exact operating arrangement.
2. Raw meat/seafood production is permitted.
3. Authentic CKT heat is achievable repeatedly.
4. Peak hot-equipment access is reliable.
5. Required refrigerated/frozen/dry storage is available.
6. Month-to-month or low-commitment launch terms.
7. All-in cost is transparent.

Price alone does not override a failed HALAL, food-quality, or production-capacity gate.
