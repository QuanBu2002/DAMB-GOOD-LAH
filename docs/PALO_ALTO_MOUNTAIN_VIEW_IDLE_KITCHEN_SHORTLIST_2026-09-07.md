# DAMB GOOD LAH — Palo Alto + Mountain View Idle-Kitchen Shortlist — 2026-09-07

## Objective
Identify restaurants/cafes in Palo Alto, Mountain View, and immediately adjacent areas whose public service hours create recurring windows for DAMB GOOD LAH to operate under a properly documented rental-kitchen arrangement.

Important: public dining hours do not prove kitchen-idle hours. Prep, catering, cleaning, receiving, hotel operations, private events, and staff call times must be confirmed directly.

## Search rule
Prior pork/non-halal use is **not** a major ranking penalty by itself. Assume DAMB GOOD LAH will use a rigorous documented sanitation/reset protocol and dedicated smallwares/storage as needed. Final arrangement still must satisfy County requirements and the chosen HALAL certifier.

Primary ranking factors:
1. recurring dark-hour window;
2. likelihood of real commercial hot-line capability;
3. owner/operator willingness;
4. storage availability;
5. packing/prep space;
6. price;
7. ability to support two wok positions or an approved equivalent;
8. sanitation/reset and regulatory fit.

# E. Questions that decide whether a candidate is real

1. What time does kitchen staff actually arrive/leave?
2. Are there recurring weekdays when the full hot line is truly unused?
3. Would ownership sign a County-compliant rental-kitchen agreement for a separate insured food business?
4. Hood length and available hot-line positions.
5. Gas burner output / wok capability / permission for approved portable or installed wok equipment.
6. Fryer access and oil-reset arrangement.
7. Dedicated labeled fridge/freezer/dry storage.
8. 6-ft raw prep table plus 6-ft component prep table or equivalent.
9. 8–12 linear feet for packing/assembly.
10. Cleanup/reset expectations and handoff time to host staff.
11. Monthly fixed price for a recurring block rather than hourly ad-hoc rental.
12. Whether County and HALAL certifier inspection/audit is acceptable.
