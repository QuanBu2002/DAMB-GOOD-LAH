## 6. HFSAA / CERTIFIER PREFLIGHT

**Goal:** determine whether DAMB GOOD LAH can be certified in a shared commercial kitchen **before** signing a long lease.

Start with HFSAA; compare IFANCA if needed.

HFSAA:
- info@hfsaa.org
- https://www.hfsaa.org/

Give them the operating concept:
- fully halal inventory; no pork owned/used by DAMB GOOD LAH
- halal-certified chicken and beef
- seafood
- certified halal chicken lap cheong
- shared commercial kitchen where other tenants may use non-halal ingredients
- wok/range + fryer + griddle + rice cooker + shared cold/dry storage
- catering/direct pickup/delivery

Ask:
1. Can a restaurant/caterer operating from a multi-tenant commissary obtain certification under their standard?
2. What rules apply if another tenant uses pork?
3. Must fryer/oil be dedicated?
4. Must wok/range/griddle tools or surfaces be dedicated?
5. Required sanitation/reset between tenants?
6. Required refrigerator/freezer/dry-storage segregation?
7. Required receiving/labeling controls?
8. Required supplier/certificate records?
9. Required ingredient review for soy sauces, kecap, fish sauce, belacan, ghee and wrappers?
10. Staff training requirements?
11. Audit frequency + startup/annual fees?
12. Exact public wording/logo permitted after approval?
