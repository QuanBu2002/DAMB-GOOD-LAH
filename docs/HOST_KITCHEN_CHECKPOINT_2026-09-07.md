# DAMB GOOD LAH — Host Kitchen Checkpoint — 2026-09-07

## Current search thesis

Prioritize **small, practical Asian kitchens with native wok capability and weekday daytime gaps** over polished destination restaurants.

Primary filters:
1. real wok burner / wok well capability and heat recovery;
2. Monday-Friday daytime access, with four reliable weekdays considered sufficient;
3. small independent / owner-operated profile;
4. recurring fixed monthly off-hour arrangement;
5. dedicated fridge/freezer/dry storage;
6. fryer/range/prep/packing compatibility;
7. County rental-kitchen paperwork and certifier acceptance.

Prior pork/non-halal use is not itself a ranking penalty; DAMB GOOD LAH assumes a rigorous documented sanitation/reset protocol, with final acceptance dependent on the certifier.

## Negotiation posture

Sell dead-hour monetization, not hourly commissary rental.
- fixed recurring block;
- fixed monthly payment;
- no dining-room use required;
- DAMB GOOD LAH brings its own labor, ingredients, insurance and smallwares;
- documented cleaning/reset;
- leave before host staff need the line.

Initial economic target remains roughly **$750-$1,500/month**, depending on weekday count, storage and equipment access, with a 60-day pilot preferred before a larger commitment.
