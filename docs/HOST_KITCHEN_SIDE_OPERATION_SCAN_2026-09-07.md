# DAMB GOOD LAH — Host Kitchen / Side-Operation Scan — 2026-09-07

## Objective

Identify kitchens where DAMB GOOD LAH could operate as a second or part-time food business without taking on a full standalone restaurant lease.

This lane is distinct from a normal commissary: the ideal arrangement is a permitted kitchen with idle capacity where DAMB GOOD LAH can secure recurring morning/lunch production blocks, segregated storage, approved HALAL controls, and permission to operate its own catering/direct-order business.

## Regulatory reality

Santa Clara County permits catering operations that prepare from a rented commercial kitchen if the operator submits the County Rental Kitchen Agreement, rental contract/lease, written operating procedures and other required materials, then passes review/inspection. A separate "host facility" pathway exists when a permitted caterer sells directly to consumers in conjunction with an approved host facility.

Therefore: a restaurant/church/venue saying "yes, use our kitchen" is not enough. Before launch, the exact agreement must be accepted by County Environmental Health and the HALAL certifier.

## Host-restaurant cold-outreach strategy

Once the explicit rental targets are exhausted, the next prospect class should be **full-service kitchens that are idle during our production window**, particularly:
- nightlife/bar/event concepts opening late afternoon/evening
- banquet/event venues with weekday morning idle kitchens
- caterers with spare weekday production capacity
- restaurants closed 1–2 weekdays or not serving breakfast/lunch

Do not pitch "sublease your restaurant." Pitch:

> DAMB GOOD LAH is a small fully halal Malaysian office-lunch/catering concept seeking a recurring permitted kitchen block approximately 7am–2pm on selected weekdays. We provide our own staff, ingredients, insurance, sanitation SOPs and permit paperwork. We are looking for dedicated storage and approved hot-line access, not dining-room use. We would pay a fixed recurring monthly kitchen-use fee.

That frames the offer as monetizing idle kitchen hours without disrupting their core business.

## Economic target for a host-kitchen deal

A side-operation kitchen only beats Snell if the all-in economics and access are better.

Working target bands:
- **excellent:** <= $600/month for ~20 recurring hours + basic storage
- **strong:** $600–$850/month if equipment/access/storage are materially better than Snell
- **workable:** $850–$1,100 only if it solves wok/HALAL/peak-access problems
- **weak:** > $1,100 for ~20 hours unless private/semi-private configuration materially reduces risk or enables additional revenue

The value of a host arrangement can justify a modest premium if it gives DAMB GOOD LAH:
- a real wok line
- dedicated fryer/oil
- better HALAL segregation
- larger refrigerator/freezer allocation
- recurring weekday lunch blocks without reservation competition
- direct pickup/retail permissions
