# DAMB GOOD LAH

**Malaysian-inspired, delivery-first ghost-kitchen project**

This repository is the durable project archive for DAMB GOOD LAH: culinary development, menu architecture, test-kitchen records, operating design, delivery testing, economics, regulatory work, and launch planning.

## Canonical brand

The canonical project and customer-facing name is **DAMB GOOD LAH**. Legacy working names `DANG GOOD LAH` and `DAMN GOOD LAH` are retired. Current artifacts have been migrated to the canonical brand while preserving their version history.

## Operating principles already solved

- Simplify operations without flattening food identity.
- Preserve four distinct flavor lanes: Padang curry, house sambal with dish-specific finish, Mamak-style mee goreng sauce, and protected char kway teow seasoning/chile/high-heat execution.
- One main chicken preparation serves multiple dishes with different final formats.
- One shrimp purchasing specification may support CKT and future shrimp dishes, with different portioning.
- Yellow noodles are shared where appropriate; CKT retains fresh flat rice noodles.
- Shared potato prep serves mee goreng body and crisp finish.
- One firm tofu SKU serves tender and crisp applications.
- Chicken skin, beef-fat shallot crisp, and ikan bilis-peanut crunch should enter service crisp and portion-ready where safe/validated, limiting service-time fryer demand.
- CKT is a protected benchmark dish. If the facility cannot provide adequate burner heat and recovery, CKT does not launch from that facility rather than being simplified into generic noodles.
- Delivery quality is part of the product: 0/15/30-minute texture, temperature, packaging, and carry testing remain mandatory.

## Current economics checkpoint

`models/DAMB_GOOD_LAH_Launch_Economics_v0_1.xlsx` is the current decision model. It intentionally uses ranges instead of false precision.

The current base case assumes:
- San Jose skilled kitchen wage planning above the 2026 legal minimum
- 2 kitchen workers
- 10 sellable main-equivalents per **paid** kitchen hour including prep, service, cleanup, and dead time
- $35.50/hour effective kitchen allocation based on the currently published 20-hour commissary tier
- 8% food waste/spoilage reserve
- $1.35 all-in packaging per main
- current marketplace fee ranges

The model is designed to be overwritten by actual invoices, measured yields, labor logs, packaging quotes, and service throughput.

## Evidence discipline

Prototype recipes, portion sizes, prices and operational thresholds are development assumptions until physically measured. Missing cost/yield data means **unknown**, not zero. Commercial decisions should use usable finished yield, landed supplier cost, economic labor, kitchen time, packaging, waste and channel fees.
